A ten question autism screening for adults

You read ten statements about how you notice things, focus, and read other people. For each one, you say how well it fits you. At the end you get a score out of 10 and a plain explanation of what it means.

Who it’s for
Adults aged 16 and over, answering about themselves
Time
About three minutes
Privacy
Your answers aren’t saved anywhere. Close the tab and they’re gone.

A screening can suggest whether a full assessment is worth asking for. It can’t tell you whether you are autistic.
